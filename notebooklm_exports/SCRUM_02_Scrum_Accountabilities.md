# Professional Scrum Master — Module 2: Scrum Accountabilities

## The Scrum Master

The Scrum Master is accountable for establishing Scrum as defined in the Scrum Guide. They do this by helping everyone understand Scrum theory and practice. The 2020 Guide made a crucial language update: the Scrum Master is no longer a "servant-leader", but a **"true leader who serves"** the Scrum Team and the larger organization.

**Accountabilities:**

- Coaching the team members in self-management and cross-functionality.
- Helping the Scrum Team focus on creating high-value Increments that meet the Definition of Done.
- Causing the removal of impediments to the Scrum Team’s progress.
- Ensuring that all Scrum events take place and are positive, productive, and kept within the timebox.

**⚓ Anchor:** The Scrum Master is the team's mechanic. They don't drive the car (that's the Developers) and they don't pick the destination (that's the Product Owner). They ensure the engine is running perfectly, remove debris from the road, and make sure everyone is following the rules of the road.

---

## The Product Owner

The Product Owner (PO) is accountable for maximizing the value of the product resulting from the work of the Scrum Team. How this is done varies widely across organizations.

**Crucial PSM 1 Facts:**
- The PO is **one person**, not a committee. They may represent the needs of a committee, but those wanting to change the Product Backlog can do so only by trying to convince the PO.
- They are solely accountable for effective Product Backlog management (creating items, ordering items, ensuring it is transparent).
- Only the Product Owner has the authority to **cancel a Sprint**.

**Exam Tip (PSM 1):** If an exam question asks "Who can cancel a Sprint—", the answer is ALWAYS the Product Owner. If it asks "Who is responsible for the Product Goal—", it is the Product Owner. Never choose "Management" or "The Scrum Master".

## The Developers

Developers are the people in the Scrum Team that are committed to creating any aspect of a usable Increment each Sprint. Note the 2020 update: the term "Development Team" was removed. It is now just "Developers" as an accountability within the cohesive Scrum Team to reduce "us vs. them" silos.

**Accountabilities:**
- Creating a plan for the Sprint (the Sprint Backlog).
- Instilling quality by adhering to a Definition of Done.
- Adapting their plan each day toward the Sprint Goal.
- Holding each other accountable as professionals.

**⚓ Anchor:** "Developer" in Scrum doesn't just mean a software engineer. If you are building an ad campaign, the copywriter and designer are the "Developers." They are the ones doing the actual work of the Sprint.
