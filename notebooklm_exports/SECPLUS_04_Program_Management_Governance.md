# CompTIA Security+ — Module 4: Program Management & Governance

## Program Management & Governance

Security isn't just firewalls; it's paperwork with teeth. **Program Management** (20% of the exam) covers the policies, compliance (like HIPAA, GDPR), and risk assessments that dictate what the firewalls should actually do.

**⚓ Anchor:** If you buy the best locks in the world but have no policy saying employees must lock the doors when they leave, you have no security. Governance is the policy. Architecture is the lock. SecOps is the alarm.

**Exam Tip (Security+):** Governance questions usually hinge on WHO is accountable, not who does the work. A Data Owner (typically a business exec) is accountable for classifying data; a Data Custodian (typically IT) just implements the controls the owner requires.
