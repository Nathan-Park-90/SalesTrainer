# Professional Scrum Master — Module 5: CSM & Practical Facilitation

## Resolving Conflict & Impediments

While the PSM 1 focuses strictly on rules, the CSM emphasizes your role as a facilitator. When the Developers are in conflict over architecture, your job is not to choose the architecture. Your job is to facilitate a structured decision-making process.

**Impediment Removal:** A true Scrum Master doesn't just fix problems; they teach the team to fix their own problems. Only step in to resolve an impediment if the Developers cannot resolve it themselves. If the environment (management, tools, outside teams) is the blocker, that is when the Scrum Master escalates and shields the team.

**⚓ Anchor:** If the team is arguing over React vs. Angular, stay out of the technical weeds. Ask: "What are our objective criteria for this choice—" Make them build a matrix. You own the process of deciding, they own the decision.

## Scaling Scrum (Briefly)

When one product requires more than 10 people, you don't grow the Scrum Team. You create multiple Scrum Teams that share the **same Product Backlog**, the **same Product Owner**, and the **same Product Goal**. Frameworks like Nexus or LeSS manage the integration between these teams.
