# CompTIA A+ — Module 5: Operational Procedures

## Operational Procedures

Professionalism, documentation, and safety. This covers ESD (Electrostatic Discharge) straps, ticketing systems, change management, and how to talk to frustrated end-users without losing your job.

**⚓ Anchor:** An ESD strap looks like theater until the one time it isn't — a static discharge you can't even feel (as low as 10-30 volts) is enough to silently kill a modern CPU or RAM stick that never shows a symptom until it fails three weeks later. Ground yourself before you touch the components, not after you remember you should have.

**Exam Tip (A+):** Professionalism and change-management questions aren't filler — they show up constantly. The “correct” answer to “a frustrated user is yelling at you” is almost always: don't argue, actively listen, and avoid being judgmental — not “explain why they're wrong.”
